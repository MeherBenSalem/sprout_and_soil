# Sprout & Soil 1.0.2

### Fixed
- **Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3:** servers with Sprout & Soil no longer fail to start. The fruit tree data used an older
  format that these versions refuse. This affected both Fabric and NeoForge.
- **Minecraft 26.3:** recipes unlock again as you collect ingredients.

### Unchanged
- Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge) and 1.21.1 (Fabric, NeoForge) play exactly as in 1.0.1.
