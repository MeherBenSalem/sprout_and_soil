# Sprout & Soil 1.0.1

### New
- Sprout & Soil now runs on **Minecraft 26.2** and **Minecraft 26.3**, on both **Fabric** and **NeoForge**. All 40
  crops, the 12 fruit trees, wild gardens, the cooking pot, cutting boards, knives, the mortar & pestle, every food,
  drink, crate and tool, and the advancements come along.

### Good to know on 26.2 and 26.3
- There is no JEI integration on these versions yet.
- Farmers and wandering traders don't get the Sprout & Soil trades there. Seeds still come from wild gardens and
  grass, and saplings from fruit tree leaves.

### Unchanged
- Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge) and 1.21.1 (Fabric, NeoForge) play exactly as in 1.0.0.
