# Sprout & Soil — Art Style Guide

All textures are **generated by code** in `tools/assetgen/sas/art/` so they stay consistent,
reviewable and re-runnable. The bar is *production quality*: every sprite should look like it
belongs next to vanilla Minecraft items, read clearly at 1x in a hotbar slot, and be distinct
from its neighbours. No placeholders, no flat single-colour blobs, no noise-filled squares.

## Canvas & format
* Items and block faces: **16x16 RGBA PNG**. GUI: 256x256. Mob effect icons: 18x18. Logo: 128x128.
* Fully transparent background (`alpha=0`). Only alpha 0 or 255 for items and cutout blocks
  (no semi-transparency: Minecraft's cutout renderer would drop it).
* Never copy pixels from vanilla or other mods. Vanilla textures may be *looked at* for scale,
  palette brightness and outline conventions (extracted reference copy:
  `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-sprout-and-soil/45a18861-1f5f-5ccb-a646-fe32596973ba/scratchpad/src/vassets/assets/minecraft/textures/`).

## Lighting & shading
* Single light source from the **top-left**. Highlights top-left, core shadow bottom-right.
* Use hue-shifted ramps (`core.ramp`, `core.shade`): shadows drift toward blue/purple,
  highlights toward yellow. 3-5 tones per material. Avoid pillow shading (darkening every edge
  equally) — shade by form.
* Specular glints (1-2 px, near-white tinted to the material) only on glossy things: tomatoes,
  peppers, eggplants, cherries, glass, metal.
* Matte things (bread, potatoes, cloth, wood) get no glint.

## Outlines (items)
* Items get a **1px outline** that is a dark, saturated, hue-shifted version of the adjacent
  colour (`Sprite.outline(lambda c: shade(c, -0.38))`) — not pure black. Vanilla-style: the
  outline is darkest at the bottom-right, can be slightly lighter on the top-left.
* Block textures (crops, leaves, crates) do **not** get item outlines; crop plants are drawn
  like vanilla crops with darker leaf edges instead.

## Composition
* Items occupy roughly 12-14 px of the 16 px canvas, centred, leaving ≥1 px margin for the
  outline. Elongated items (knives, leeks, cucumbers, bananas) run diagonally bottom-left →
  top-right like vanilla tools.
* Silhouettes first: each item must be identifiable by silhouette alone. Siblings (e.g. the
  four sickles, juices) share a silhouette but differ in material ramp.
* Containers are consistent across the mod:
  * **bowl**: same wooden bowl (vanilla-like proportions) at the bottom; food piled above the
    rim with 2-3 visible ingredient chunks;
  * **plate**: white ceramic plate with pale blue rim seen at a 3/4 angle (ellipse, ~14x6),
    food on top;
  * **mug**: terracotta-red mug with handle on the right;
  * **bottle** drinks: vanilla-glass-bottle silhouette filled with the drink colour, cork on top;
  * **jar** (jams, pickles, peanut butter): wider squat glass jar with a lid/cloth cover.

## Crops (block textures)
* Drawn on a 16x16 canvas, **bottom-aligned** (plants grow from y=15 upward), transparent
  background, rendered with the vanilla `crop` (#-shaped) or `cross` model.
* Height progression by stage: ~4-5 px, ~8 px, ~12 px, ~15-16 px.
* Stage 0 is a sprout, stage 1 young leaves, stage 2 shows flowers or unripe produce, stage 3
  shows the ripe, clearly readable produce in its item colours.
* Leaves: 3-4 green tones with dark edges; stems a separate darker/yellower green.
* Staked crops (tomato, cucumber, green bean, peppercorn) include a thin wooden stake;
  grapes use a small trellis frame.
* Tall crops (corn): stage2/3 have an extra `_top` texture for the block above (the model is
  two blocks tall); bottom texture must connect seamlessly to the top one.

## Fruit trees
* Leaves are tileable like vanilla leaves (with transparent holes, `cube_all` cutout). Four
  stages share an identical base leaf pattern; later stages add blossoms → unripe fruit →
  ripe fruit as 2x2/2x3 clusters scattered so they tile nicely.
* Saplings: cross texture, small trunk + a leaf cluster, often with one fruit/blossom hint.

## Crates
* `crate_side`/`crate_bottom` are shared; each `<item>_crate_top` shows a wooden rim
  (2 px) around a heap of the produce drawn as small 3-5 px repeated shapes with variation.

## Cooking pot GUI (textures/gui/container/cooking_pot.png, 256x256)
Vanilla-style container panel (light grey `#c6c6c6`, white top-left bevel, dark-grey
bottom-right bevel, 1 px black-ish rounded border), at `(0,0)` size **176x166**:
* 6 input slots (18x18 vanilla slot frames, inner item position given) at item positions
  x ∈ {30, 48, 66}, y ∈ {17, 35}
* heat indicator background (unlit flame, dark) at **(49, 56)** size 14x14
* progress arrow background (empty) at **(89, 26)** size 24x17
* container ("serve in") slot at item position **(92, 54)** with a faint bowl ghost icon
* output slot at item position **(124, 30)**, drawn as a large 26x26 output frame centred on it
* player inventory: 3 rows at item positions x=8+18*i, y=84+18*r; hotbar y=142
* labels are drawn by code (title at 8,6; "Inventory" at 8,72) — leave that area plain.
Sprites elsewhere in the sheet:
* **lit flame** at `(176, 0)` size 14x14
* **full progress arrow** at `(176, 14)` size 24x17 (revealed left→right)

## 3D models
* Standard Java block-model JSON, elements inside -16..32, texture sizes 16x16 per face UV
  space, rotations only on a single axis in 22.5° steps.
* Every model sets `"textures": {"particle": ...}` and a full `display` block (gui,
  ground, fixed, thirdperson_righthand, firstperson_righthand, head) so the item looks good.
* Models face **north** (front on the north side); blockstates rotate them.
* Add `"render_type": "minecraft:cutout"` when any texture has transparent pixels.
* Keep element count reasonable (< 40) and avoid z-fighting (offset overlapping faces by 0.01+).

## Review loop
Every lane writes contact sheets to `tools/assetgen/build/previews/` and must inspect them
at scale (and 3D renders for models) before calling a lane done.
